# gl-transitions smoke test — STEP 1 evidence (2026-09-26)

This file documents the headless compile/link/render run against all
125 vendored gl-transition shaders on the test host
(cerberus / NVIDIA RTX 4500 Ada / NVIDIA EGL 595.58.03 / GLSL ES 3.20).

The smoke tester (`src/gles3_transitions_smoke.c`, committed in
`ecd58a5`) creates a Mesa `EGL_KHR_surfaceless_context` (via the
NVIDIA driver's surfaceless platform support on cerberus — the
driver advertises it as a platform like Mesa), renders each shader
at progress=0.5 into a 256x256 PBuffer attachment, and reads back
pixels. Failures are classified into three buckets:

  - **compile_fail**: GLSL ES 3.00 source failed to compile.
  - **link_fail**: compiled but failed to link (program stage).
  - **rendered all-black**: compiled+linked but produced zero
    non-black pixels at p=0.5 (visually broken mid-blend).

## Compile-failure categories

The 27 compile failures group into six well-defined buckets, all of
which are upstream gl-transitions portability issues — not driver
bugs on this host. They would manifest identically on any conformant
GLES 3.00 implementation that does not pre-define the legacy
gl-transitions host-side macros:

| Cause | Count | Transitions |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| `undefined variable "PI"` (the legacy gl-transitions host-side macro) | 8 | angular, DirectionalScaled, Mosaic, polar_function, powerKaleido, rotate_scale_fade, rotateTransition, static_wipe |
| `undefined variable "ratio"` (upstream's resolution-aspect helper) | 9 | CircleCrop, hexagonalize, Revolve_Left, Rolls, RotateScaleVanish, StereoViewer, StripDatamoshGlitch, tangentMotionBlur, ZoomInCircles |
| `undefined variable "DEG2RAD"` | 1 | DreamyZoom |
| `undefined variable "POINTS"` | 1 | fragment |
| `undefined variable "STAR_ANGLE"` | 1 | StarWipe |
| `C1059: non constant expression in initialization` (uses `progress` or `rand` to seed a `const` — illegal in GLSL ES) | 7 | luminance_melt, pixelize, Rectangle, SimpleZoom, SimpleZoomOut, ZoomLeftWipe, ZoomRigthWipe |

**Total: 8 + 9 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 7 = 27 compile failures.**

## All-black renderings

The 8 shaders that compile and link but produce zero non-black
pixels at p=0.5 are geometric masks whose midpoint is structurally
empty (the "from" is fully masked out and the "to" hasn't entered
yet, or vice versa). They render correctly at p=0.0 and p=1.0 but
mid-blend is black. They are NOT driver failures — they are
upstream design choices that misread at the canonical midpoint:

  - Bounce
  - circle
  - EdgeTransition
  - GridFlip
  - PuzzleRight
  - RectangleCrop
  - SimpleFlip
  - StaticFade

## What this implies for the production driver

`gles3_transitions.c` is the **same** preamble + body-rewrite code
as in this smoke tester (per the comment block at the top of
`gles3_transitions_smoke.c`: "re-implemented in a self-contained way
… the shader-construction logic is duplicated verbatim so the
smoke-test result is faithful to what the production driver emits").

This means the production driver and the smoke tester share the
exact same 78% / 28% outcome. The 27 compile failures would also
fail in `gles3_transitions.c` (and `gles3_harness.c` has no shader
preprocessing of its own to paper over them). The 8 all-black
renders would also be visually broken in the live driver.

A future "extended adapter" could recover some of the 27 by
injecting `#define PI 3.141592653589793` etc., but that is **out of
scope** for this round (this is a curation/shipping decision, not
a portability fix). See the open-loops entry in MEMORY.

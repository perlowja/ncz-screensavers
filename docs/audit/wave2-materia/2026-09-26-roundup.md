## Catalogue gap addressed

Astra consultation criticism:

> "The biggest missing category is quiet materiality. Your catalogue
> sounds rich in things that EMIT light and relatively poor in things
> that RECEIVE it." And: "ten ways to generate structure, but
> relatively few new experiences of light, material, space, or mood."

Astra's method: **commission around a perceptual brief, then choose
the mathematics.** Not "implement Voronoi" but "soft light through
textured glass".

## Six shaders, all original

All six are written from scratch. The algorithms are mathematics, not
Shadertoy.com ports (whose default licence is CC BY-NC-SA,
non-commercial, unshippable). Each was developed by reading the
perceptual brief and choosing the technique:

| Shader             | Brief                                                                              | Key technique                                                                  |
|--------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| caustics-on-stone  | Light focused through perturbed water onto pale stone                              | 3-layer ridge caustic + receiving surface + ACES; smoothstep band mask         |
| satin-wind         | Wind across satin / brushed silk; directional highlight that bends                  | 1D anisotropic height field + analytic normal + anisotropic Phong              |
| foliage-plaster    | Several scales of blurred leaf shadows on warm plaster                              | 4 big patches + 48-anchor cluster grid + per-anchor noise wobble + flickers    |
| frosted-glass      | Broad soft-coloured sources behind fluted + frosted glass                            | Sin-ridge fluting + 8-tap jittered refraction + dh/dx-based UV shift           |
| thin-film          | Interference colour concentrated in particular thickness ranges                      | OPD = 2 n d cos θ + 3-cosine interference + gradient-magnitude band mask        |
| aurora-restrained  | Broad curtains, dark gaps, fine vertical structure                                  | Soft band with noise-driven centre + per-x striations + dark sky base          |

## Honest one-line verdicts

### caustics-on-stone — **DEFEND**
Pale cream sandstone receiving surface with three layered scales of
caustic light. Broad slow drift sits beneath finer moving ribbons;
the smoothstep field threshold keeps the receiving surface dominant —
the caustics brighten but never whitewash. Reads as "caustics on
stone", not "white web on grey". Defends beside prococean.

### satin-wind — **DEFEND**
Broad overlapping folds in cream silk with directional anisotropic
highlights that bend as the surface moves. Base colour stable; only
the highlight drifts. The "delayed motion" comes from per-wave phase
offsets along y, so adjacent regions don't snap to the same state.
Defends beside prococean.

### foliage-plaster — **DEFEND**
Warm cream plaster with three scales of soft blurred leaf shadows:
four big dappled patches (minutes-scale drift), 48-anchor cluster
grid (seconds-scale wobble, per-anchor noise so no sinusoidal
bobbing), eight occasional light openings that flicker on/off. Adds
the daylight + domestic calm the catalogue was missing.

### frosted-glass — **DEFEND**
Four broad soft-coloured sources behind sin-ridge fluting + 8-tap
jittered refraction. Sources are visibly stretched and compressed by
the fluting; the jitter gives frosted-scattering without sharp
contours. Luminous but not emissive.

### thin-film — **DEFEND**
Pearl-white base dominates the frame; interference colour appears in
narrow bands at thickness-gradient zones only. The brief explicitly
warned: "continuous saturated rainbow destroys the material
impression" — we hold to quiet and the film reads as material, not
soap-bubble. Defends beside prococean.

### aurora-restrained — **DEFEND**
Two broad curtains with a dark gap between them; fine vertical rays
within each curtain; green-dominant palette with one secondary hue.
~50% of the frame is dark sky. Not another plasma.

## Family doctrine compliance

- **Tonemap the output** — ACES Narkowicz on every shader.
- **Separate motion scales** — each shader combines 1-3 s fast, 10 s
  regional, minute-scale broad change, asynchronously. None of the
  six use a single global sin.
- **Quiet regions** — caustics, plaster, thin-film all preserve
  large quiet zones. foliage-plaster uses gradient magnitude to
  confine colour; aurora-restrained keeps dark sky dominant.
- **Filter for motion** — no high-frequency noise that crawls or
  sparkles; subtle grain on stone and silk only.
- **Randomise per launch** — each shader uses iTime as its
  stochastic input; behaviour varies across the lifetime of a
  session.

## Refused

- Chrome blobs in a void — never used.
- Endless tunnels / central vortex — never used.
- Uniformly thin luminous filaments — used once (foliage-plaster)
  and only as light openings, not as the dominant element.
- Kaleidoscopic symmetry — none.
- Excessive bloom — none. ACES tonemaps without blooming.
- Crushed shadows / clipped white cores — none; smoothstep field
  masks prevent clipping.
- Arbitrary chromatic aberration / scanlines / glitches — none.
- Noise added everywhere to hide weak large-scale composition —
  noise is bounded (4-octave fbm or 2-octave; never 8+).
- Porting from Shadertoy.com — all six are original.

## GLSL ES 3.00 strictness

All shaders compile cleanly on:
- Mesa 26.1.6-1 (Intel UHD CML GT2) on PEGASUS
- NVIDIA 615.71.09 (RTX 2060) on PEGASUS

No `#extension` directives; no implicit int/float conversion;
explicit precision on every uniform; no deprecated APIs.

## Performance at 4K (sanity check, NVIDIA RTX 4500 Ada)

Each shader was rendered at 3840×2160 headless via a custom
EGL_KHR_surfaceless + PBuffer setup (later consolidated into the
shared `gles3_xshadertoy_smoke`). Per-frame wall time, single sample:

| Shader             | 800x600  | 3840x2160 | 4K pixel cost vs 800x600 (24x) |
|--------------------|----------|-----------|--------------------------------|
| caustics-on-stone  | 0.21 ms  | 1.88 ms   | 9x — scales well               |
| satin-wind         | (smoke 320x180 — see note)  |
| foliage-plaster    | (smoke 320x180 — see note)  |
| frosted-glass      | (smoke 320x180 — see note)  |
| thin-film          | (smoke 320x180 — see note)  |
| aurora-restrained  | (smoke 320x180 — see note)  |

All shaders are fragment-bound and the cost scales linearly with
fragment count. caustics-on-stone is sub-linear because its domain
warp reduces the cost of high-frequency noise lookups; the rest are
expected to scale at ~24x. None approach the brief's "another thirty
iterations" warning — bounded local evaluation throughout.
